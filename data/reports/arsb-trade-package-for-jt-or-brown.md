## Bottom line
- **Plan A:** Offer Amon-Ra St. Brown for Jonathan Taylor. Even 1-for-1 is fair. Ask for a bench add-on first and drop it if they say no.
- **Plan B:** Amon-Ra for Chase Brown **plus** a real WR2 (a top-30 type). Don't trade him for Brown alone.
- **Before you send it:** Amon-Ra left Sunday's game vs the Vikings to be checked for a concussion, but he came back the next series. Check his practice reports Wed–Fri. If he's limited, the other managers will offer less.
- **Hold** Kyren Williams. He's your RB1 either way.

## 📈 What the trade charts say

| Amon-Ra St. Brown | 3,542 (WR4) | Sell high |

- Amon-Ra for JT is about even. You have roughly 270 points of value to work with, so ask for a bench WR or handcuff.
- Amon-Ra for Brown alone leaves about 1,640 points behind. That's around a WR2 (Tucker or Waddle level) the other manager should add.

## 🏈 RB check
- **Jonathan Taylor:** I couldn't confirm his 2026 numbers. One preseason outlook called him a top-5 RB and the RB1 in fantasy points last year. One page mentioned Colts trade talk, but it looked out of date, so I'd ignore it. The Colts play Dallas on Sunday.
- **Chase Brown:** Week 8 vs Tennessee he had 12 carries for 55 yards and caught 8 of 9 targets for 46 yards. He also lost a fumble. Season to date: 340 rushing yards, 1 TD, 189 receiving yards. That's a weak scoring rate, but backup Samaje Perine is little threat, so the volume is his. In 2025 he started slow and was RB6 from Week 6 on. Week 9 is vs Atlanta in Madrid. One source says the Bengals' bye is Week 10, which I couldn't confirm.

## 🔥 What this does to your lineup
- **Right now:** Kyren Williams, Blake Corum and Rachaad White (at FLEX) is thin for a 16-team, 1-FLEX league, and James Conner is on IR.
- **With JT:** JT and Kyren start at RB and Corum moves to FLEX. That's a big upgrade.
- **Losing Amon-Ra** leaves Wan'Dale Robinson plus Waddle, Tucker and Downs at WR. Start the two best each week. You can live with that because the RB gain is bigger.
- **Note:** The league data I had was cut off, so I can't see who owns JT or Brown or what WRs they have. Look at their rosters for the add-on piece. If one of them is weak at WR, that's the best manager to target.
