## Bottom line
- 🚨 **Bench Watson in Carbon Arc and EPIC.** The Packers are on bye this week. Their next game is Wednesday, Nov. 25 at the Rams.
- 🚨 **The Rams are also on bye in Week 11.** In 🐝🐀 that takes out Kyren Williams and Blake Corum. In Carbon Arc it takes out Tyler Higbee. Swap all of them.
- ✅ **Start Amon-Ra in 🐝🐀.** He was questionable with a rib injury, played anyway, and had 4 catches for 98 yards and 2 TDs in Munich.
- ⏳ **Keep Evans on IR in Carbon Arc.** He hasn't practiced yet. Activate him only once he's practicing fully.

- In 🐝🐀, Waddle and Woody Marks are listed Out and Conner is on IR. That leaves your RB room very thin this week. It's one more reason to push your Amon-Ra trade for a running back. Check waivers for a 1-week RB too.
- In EPIC, Drake London and Burden are both listed questionable. I couldn't confirm either player's status, so check their practice reports on Wednesday and Thursday.

## 🚑 Amon-Ra St. Brown (DET)
- He has a rib injury. He was limited in practice and listed questionable for Week 10, then was active and played.
- He played through the same injury in Week 9 too (8 catches, 86 yards).
- If he gets a questionable tag again this week, it's not a reason to sit him. He has played through it twice in a row.

## 📈 Watson's season so far
- Week 10 was quiet: 3 catches for 37 yards in a 30-10 loss to Minnesota.
- Season total: 52 catches, 916 yards and 7 TDs. He's still a must-start in Week 12.

## 🚑 Mike Evans (SF)
- He has Achilles tendinitis and has missed SF's last three games.
- On Monday, Kyle Shanahan said they're "hoping" to get him back at practice this week. SF plays the Vikings in Mexico City on Sunday.
- No practice report has come out yet. If he's limited by Friday, he's a risky start in his first game back. Keep him on IR and don't drop anyone yet.
