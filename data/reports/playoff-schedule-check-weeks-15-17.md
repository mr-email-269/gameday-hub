## Bottom line
- **Hold Justin Jefferson and Aaron Jones.** One preseason study gave Minnesota the easiest playoff schedule of all 32 teams: DET, WAS, NYJ.
- **Plan around Brock Purdy (Meech, Carbon Arc).** SF faces LAC, KC and PHI, one of the hardest playoff schedules. Look for a backup QB by Week 14.
- **Look for a TE upgrade over Cade Otton.** TB faces NO, ATL and LAR, which grades as a hard schedule. Juwan Johnson (NO: TB, ARI, ATL) is a better playoff bet.
- **In 🐝🐀, Kyren Williams and Blake Corum are fine to hold.** The Rams get DAL in Week 15, but Seattle in Week 16 is a tough game.

## 📈 Good playoff schedules
- **MIN** (Jefferson, A. Jones): DET, WAS, NYJ. Best in the league in one ranking.
- **KC** (Kelce, K. Walker): NE, SF, then LAC indoors. Also graded a top-five schedule.
- **PIT** (Germie Bernard): BAL, CAR, TEN. Easy defenses.
- **IND** (Josh Downs): TEN, CIN, CLE. Easy schedule overall.
- **NO** (Juwan Johnson, Kamara): TB, ARI, ATL. One of the easiest schedules in ESPN's study.
- **DAL** (Javonte Williams): LAR, JAX, NYG. All three games are indoors.

## 🚑 Tough playoff schedules
- **SF** (Purdy, Mike Evans): LAC, KC, PHI.
- **TB** (Otton): NO, ATL, LAR. Graded hard.
- **WAS** (Diggs, Antonio Williams): ATL, MIN, JAX. Graded hard by NBC, easy by ESPN. That split makes Diggs a fine trade chip in Meech.
- **LV** (Jeanty, Mike Washington, Tre Tucker): DEN, TEN, ARI. Denver is a tough game in Week 15.

**Defenses:** Kansas City's defense (Carbon Arc) and the Jets' defense (EPIC: ARI, NE, MIN) are OK. Carolina's defense (CIN, PIT, SEA) is shaky in Week 15, so stream one that week.

## ⚠️ Caveats
- These grades come from preseason studies that use last year's defenses. Defenses change, and Minnesota's was ranked #1 after Week 6. Check matchups again in Week 13.
- Sources disagree on some home/away details, so I listed opponents only.
- Week 17 only matters if your leagues use a Weeks 15-17 playoff. Sleeper starts playoffs in Week 15 by default.
