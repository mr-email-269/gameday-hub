# How the trade deadline works in Sleeper

**Short answer:** trades lock at the deadline your commissioner set, usually around week 11.

## What to check
- League settings → *Trade deadline* shows the week.
- Pending trades still need to process before it passes.
- Review period: 1–2 days if your league uses vetoes.

## What to do now
1. Sell players whose schedule gets hard after week 12.
2. Buy players with easy playoff matchups.
