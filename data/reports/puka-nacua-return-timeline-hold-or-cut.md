## 🚑 What happened
- He hurt his right knee (MCL) and his toe (turf toe) in Week 9 against Washington.
- The Rams put him on IR on Nov. 12.
- Team doctors then did surgery on the MCL. The rest of the knee came back clean.
- **New timeline: 4-5 months to recover.** ESPN reported on Nov. 21 that he'll miss the rest of the regular season and the playoffs.
- Coach Sean McVay said a playoff return is something the Rams "haven't thought about."
- The NFL also suspended him for 3 games for breaking its conduct policy. He's serving that while on IR, so it changes nothing for fantasy.
- Before the injury he had 70 catches, 715 yards and 4 TDs in 7 games. That's elite, but it no longer matters for this season.
