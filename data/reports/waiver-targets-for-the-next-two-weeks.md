## Bottom line
- 🔥 **Add Omar Cooper Jr. (WR, NYJ)** in all four leagues if he's free. He's the top waiver add this week. The 🐝🐀 and Invitational teams need him most.
- 🏈 **🐝🐀: add Roschon Johnson or Jawhar Jordan** for RB depth. Washington has a bye in Week 7, so Rachaad White and Antonio Williams can't play. Start White again in Week 8.
- 📅 **Get a TE for Week 8.** The Saints play Week 7, then have a bye. Juwan Johnson starts at TE for you in 🐝🐀, Carbon Arc and EPIC. Grab Chig Okonkwo (WAS) now, while his team is on bye and nobody's looking at him. In EPIC, Terrance Ferguson comes off your IR in Week 8, so that league is covered.
- 🚑 **Invitational:** Parker Washington (JAX), Keon Coleman (BUF) and Stefon Diggs (WAS) all have Week 7 byes. Pick up Ja'Kobi Lane or Jaylin Noel to fill a WR spot.

## ✅ You already have these breakouts
- **Carbon Arc:** Mike Washington Jr. was the #1 add at Yahoo this week. He's playing more and more (35% of snaps) behind Ashton Jeanty, who isn't running well. You also have Kendre Miller (20 carries in Week 6, a career high) and Dameon Pierce (Saquon Barkley has a hamstring injury). Keep all three.
- **🐝🐀:** Antonio Williams leads Washington's WRs in snaps now that McLaurin is out. Rachaad White played 70.5% of the snaps in Week 6. Keep both through the bye.
- **Germie Bernard** (Invitational, Carbon Arc) is on this week's lists of WRs to add. Keep him.
